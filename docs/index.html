    How the numbers work

    Hours
    Each reviewer's total playtime on Steam for that game, as of today. Not just when they wrote the review.
    Addiction score
    Median hours × (1 + share of reviewers who played in the last two weeks). The median keeps a few people who left the game idling for months from skewing things, and the second part rewards games people keep coming back to.
    Age-adjusted
    Older games collect more hours simply because people have had longer to play them. So each game's addiction score is compared with the 30 games in the list closest to it in age. 2.0× means people play it twice as much as a typical game its age, 1.0× means about average. Age counts from when the game first became playable on Steam, Early Access included.
    Still playing
    Share of the sampled reviewers who played at least a few minutes in the last two weeks.
    Heavy players
    Share of reviewers with 100+ hours.
    Hours after reviewing
    Median of total hours minus hours at the time of the review. High numbers mean people kept playing long after giving their opinion.
    Keep in mind
    Recent reviewers aren't every player, and playtime only counts time spent in the Steam version. Favourites and hidden games are saved in this browser only.
