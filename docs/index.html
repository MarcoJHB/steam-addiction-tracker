    How the numbers work

    Hours
    Each reviewer's total playtime on Steam for that game, as of today. Not just when they wrote the review.
    Addiction score
    Median hours × (1 + share of reviewers who played in the last two weeks). The median keeps a few people who left the game idling for months from skewing things, and the second part rewards games people keep coming back to.
    Still playing
    Share of the sampled reviewers who played at least a few minutes in the last two weeks.
    Heavy players
    Share of reviewers with 100+ hours.
    Hours after reviewing
    Median of total hours minus hours at the time of the review. High numbers mean people kept playing long after giving their opinion.
    Keep in mind
    Recent reviewers aren't every player. Older games skew higher because their reviewers have had years to rack up hours, and playtime only counts time spent in the Steam version.
